WORLD 1: THE RULES KINGDOM

General training guidelines

  • Follow the strict training schedule and the official training calendar.
  • If you have a conflict, communicate with Ms. Casuga first.
  • Clarifications and concerns must be raised immediately. Do not make assumptions.
  • Monitor the calendar for changes and announcements.
  • Use Discord for daily emergency briefings.
  • After training, head home immediately and remain reachable.

Attendance and allowable exceptions

The general guidelines identify non-negotiable academic commitments as the basis for the stated attendance exceptions. Coordinate the conflict with Ms. Casuga first.
  • Online Publishing: Training may proceed if 2–3 members are unavailable for a maximum of 2 hours; the deadline will be adjusted.
  • Radio Broadcasting: Training may proceed under the same conditions, but only one output will be presented or recorded.
  • If several members are unavailable or the absence exceeds the allowable period, the session will not proceed.
  • Outside the stated exceptions, all members are expected to attend.

Saturday Press Conference attendance rules

The official Saturday Press Conference simulation has separate attendance rules from general training.

REMINDER: Follow the attendance rules for the specific activity. Clarify uncertainties with the trainer.
